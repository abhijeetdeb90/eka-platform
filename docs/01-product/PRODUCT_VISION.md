# EKA Product Vision

## Working positioning

EKA is a trusted knowledge and community layer for humanity's spiritual, cultural, local, and lived experiences.

## Core principle

EKA should be a structured, trusted knowledge system that happens to have a social layer.

## Scope of knowledge

- Spirituality and religious traditions
- Cultures, customs, and communities
- Local life and tourism
- Religious and heritage places
- Food, festivals, events, art, and history
- Businesses, brands, creators, experiences, and stories

## Core loop

Source → Verify → Contextualize → Discover → Understand → Learn → Discuss → Create → Preserve.

## Product principles

1. Respect source traditions and community context.
2. Separate documented facts from interpretation and opinion.
3. Make provenance visible.
4. Allow communities to participate in trust and moderation.
5. Design for learning, not only engagement.
6. Build reusable entities and relationships rather than isolated posts.
7. Preserve cultural nuance and disagreement without promoting hostility.

## Non-goals for the first MVP

- Becoming a full replacement for YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook.
- Supporting every possible monetization mechanism.
- Automating sensitive cultural or religious verification without human oversight.

# Problem, Users, and Jobs To Be Done

## Primary user groups

1. Learners and curious explorers
2. Community members and tradition holders
3. Creators and educators
4. Pandits, astrologers, scholars, and practitioners
5. Local businesses and experience providers
6. Moderators, reviewers, and administrators
7. Brands and institutional partners

## Core problems

- Information is fragmented and difficult to contextualize.
- Authenticity and source quality are unclear.
- Cultural knowledge is often flattened into generic content.
- Creators lack a structured environment for trusted educational content.
- Communities need mechanisms to contribute context and flag harm.
- Local knowledge and experiences are difficult to discover together.

## Job statements

- Help me understand a tradition without losing its context.
- Help me discover meaningful places, people, practices, and stories.
- Help me publish knowledge with sources and attribution.
- Help me assess whether a claim is documented, interpretive, or disputed.
- Help my community correct, contextualize, or preserve knowledge.
