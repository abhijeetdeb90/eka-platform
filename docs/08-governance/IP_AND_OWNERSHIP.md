# IP Ownership and Legal Readiness

## Documentation evidence

Maintain:

- Product vision and dated decision history
- Architecture diagrams and version history
- Original design files
- Source code repository history
- Content policy and taxonomy decisions
- Contributor agreements
- Contractor and developer IP assignment agreements
- Third-party license inventory
- Brand/name search records
- Privacy and terms drafts

## Protection layers

1. Contractual ownership of commissioned work.
2. Repository and account access control.
3. Trademark evaluation and filing where appropriate.
4. Copyright protection for eligible original code, text, graphics, and documentation.
5. Confidentiality agreements for genuinely confidential information.
6. Data protection compliance and documented consent where required.

## Important

A business idea, broad concept, or feature list is generally not protected in the same way as original expression, code, brand assets, confidential information, or registrable intellectual property. Obtain qualified legal advice before relying on a protection strategy.
