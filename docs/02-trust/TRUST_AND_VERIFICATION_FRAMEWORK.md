# Trust and Verification Framework

## 1. The 5-Pillar EKA Trust Architecture

Trust in EKA is multi-dimensional and cannot be collapsed into an opaque score or a simplistic blue checkmark. The EKA Trust Architecture is structured into **5 First-Class Operational Layers**:

```
EKA TRUST ARCHITECTURE
├── 1. Human Authenticity (The Human-First Community Rule: zero bots, zero synthetic personas)
├── 2. Epistemic Verification (Explainable L0–L5 Verification Ladder)
├── 3. Source Provenance (Canonical Text, Manuscripts, Lineage Passports & Chains of Custody)
├── 4. Content Authenticity (5-Tier Epistemic Taxonomy & Strict Anti-Noise Policy)
└── 5. Community Governance (Living Tradition Keepers, Hermeneutic Arbitration & Appeals)
```

---

## 2. Pillar 1: Human Authenticity (The Human-First Rule)

> **"The intelligence is artificial. The community isn't."**  
> **"AI-powered knowledge. Human-powered community."**

- **Absolute Zero Bot Tolerance:** No bot accounts, no AI pretending to be people, and no synthetic engagement.
- **Backend vs. Frontend Boundary:** AI powers knowledge verification, translation, moderation triage, and semantic search behind the scenes. The visible community layer remains strictly human.
- **Sanctions:** Immediate account termination and forfeiture of creator monetization for synthetic engagement or bot manipulation.

## 3. Pillar 2: Epistemic Verification Ladder (L0 to L5)

Verification is never binary. EKA communicates explainable provenance across a 6-level ladder:

- **Level 0 (Unverified Contribution):** Freshly submitted reflection or claim. No citations attached.
- **Level 1 (Source-Referenced):** Contributor has attached at least one verifiable historical, canonical, or published citation.
- **Level 2 (Community Corroborated):** Verified members of the source tradition confirm the description matches living practice.
- **Level 3 (Peer / Expert Endorsed):** Accredited scholar, lineage custodian, or recognized practitioner has reviewed and signed off.
- **Level 4 (Multi-Source Corroboration):** Supported by $\ge 3$ independent canonical or academic chronicles.
- **Level 5 (Archival Benchmark):** Certified by national archives, academic bodies, or custodian institutions.

---

## 4. Pillar 3: Source Provenance & Passports

Every knowledge object must maintain a verifiable chain of origin:

- Primary source attribution (Text, manuscript, inscription, living oral lineage).
- Digital archive URI (e.g. Poona Critical Edition, UNESCO, GRETIL).
- Immutable version history and edit logs.
- Separation of commercial sponsorship from canonical attribution.

---

## 5. Pillar 4: Content Authenticity & 5-Tier Taxonomy

Contributors must tag statements according to their epistemic nature:

1. **Documented Tradition:** Backed by verifiable canonical records.
2. **Interpretation:** Hermeneutic commentary or philosophical explanation.
3. **Opinion & Reflection:** Subjective lived experience or personal insight.
4. **Living Practice:** Regional folkways and unwritten oral customs.
5. **AI-Assisted Synthesis:** Machine-assisted summaries (labeled, requiring human review).

**Strict Prohibitions:**
- **Zero Miracle Guarantee:** Guaranteed supernatural, financial, or medical outcomes are strictly banned.
- **Anti-Noise Policy:** Dopamine traps, vanity selfies, rage-bait, and engagement pods are suppressed as noise.

---

## 6. Pillar 5: Community Governance & Dispute Resolution

- **Living Tradition Keepers:** Local elders and community members who validate whether representations align with lived reality.
- **Hermeneutic Conflict Queue:** Disputed dates, translations, and regional variations are openly documented rather than erased.
- **Human Appeals Ladder:** Human oversight on all high-impact moderation decisions.

---

## 7. Prohibited Shortcut

AI-generated confidence scores must never be presented as proof of cultural, religious, historical, or factual authenticity. Machine models can assist in locating sources, but only verified human evidence confers epistemic status.
