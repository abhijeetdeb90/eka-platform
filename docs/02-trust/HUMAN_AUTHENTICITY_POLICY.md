# Human Authenticity & Zero-Bot Policy

## Core Principle

**No bot accounts. No AI pretending to be people. No synthetic engagement.**

The visible community layer of EKA is strictly human. Cultural transmission, spiritual reflection, living practice, and community dialogue require authentic human presence.

> **"The intelligence is artificial. The community isn't."**  
> **"AI-powered knowledge. Human-powered community."**

---

## Approved Behind-the-Scenes AI Capabilities

AI powers EKA as infrastructural and cognitive assistance behind the scenes:

- **Knowledge verification:** Cross-referencing claims against canonical texts, manuscripts, and archives.
- **Translation:** Converting regional languages, dialects, and scripts to broaden accessibility.
- **Moderation assistance:** Triage and detection of hate speech, slurs, harassment, fraud, and copyright issues for human review.
- **Personalization:** Recommending relevant traditions, sacred places, and topics based on member interests.
- **Search & discovery:** Semantic retrieval across the multi-dimensional Cultural Knowledge Graph.
- **Content assistance:** Assisting human creators with grammar, structural outlines, and reference suggestions.
- **Fraud & spam detection:** Flagging coordinated bot networks, credential stuffing, and velocity anomalies.
- **Community management tools:** Providing community keepers and moderators with analytics and health metrics.

---

## The Allowed vs. Not Allowed Boundary

| Permitted (AI as Human Tool) | Prohibited (Synthetic Infiltration) |
| :--- | :--- |
| AI helps a human create, format, or draft a post | AI creates an automated or fake human account |
| AI translates a person's message across languages | AI pretends to be a community member or elder |
| AI recommends relevant people, circles, and topics | AI generates fake comments, replies, or likes |
| AI helps verify historical and cultural citations | AI manufactures engagement or artificial consensus |
| AI assists human moderators in evaluating reports | AI participates covertly in discussions or debates |
| Clearly labeled, transparent official EKA AI assistant | Any "person" or account that is actually a bot |

---

## Enforcement & Sanctions

1. **Zero Warning Termination:** Accounts identified as automated bots or synthetic personas are permanently banned immediately. Unlike human infractions that follow the 4-step moderation ladder, bot accounts are not entitled to warnings.
2. **Engagement Stripping:** All likes, comments, and follows generated by synthetic accounts are expunged from platform counters and ranking algorithms.
3. **Creator Economy Forfeiture:** Any practitioner, guide, or creator found employing synthetic engagement pods or bot reviews permanently forfeits pending earnings and is barred from the EKA Marketplace.
4. **Transparent Labeling Requirement:** Any automated utility or assistive agent deployed by EKA must carry an unambiguous platform badge identifying it as machine intelligence.
