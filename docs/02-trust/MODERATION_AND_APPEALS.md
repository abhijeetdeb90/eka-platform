# Community Governance and Moderation

## Objectives

Protect respectful participation while allowing legitimate disagreement, interpretation, and historical debate.

## Moderation categories

- Spam
- Harassment
- Hate or dehumanization
- Misinformation
- Fraud/scam
- Copyright concern
- Privacy violation
- Unsafe advice
- Cultural misrepresentation
- Commercial disclosure failure

## Actions

- No action
- Request context
- Label
- Limit visibility
- Remove
- Suspend
- Escalate
- Restore after appeal

## Governance requirements

- Written policy
- Consistent enforcement
- Evidence and reason codes
- Audit trail
- Appeal mechanism
- Moderator access restrictions
- Periodic policy review
