# AI Governance, Operational Boundaries & Prompt Security

## 1. Core Operating Directive: Human-First Community

> **"The intelligence is artificial. The community isn't."**  
> **"AI-powered knowledge. Human-powered community."**

EKA establishes a strict architectural boundary: **AI powers knowledge behind the scenes, but the visible community layer stays strictly human.**

---

## 2. Approved AI Infrastructure (Behind-the-Scenes)

AI may be deployed only in service of human understanding, safety, and cultural preservation:

- **Knowledge verification & citation checking:** Cross-referencing submitted claims against authoritative digital corpora.
- **Cross-lingual translation:** Translating ancient languages, regional dialects, and living scripts with explicit translation disclosures.
- **Harm & safety moderation assistance:** Pre-screening hate speech, threats, harassment, and fraud for human moderator review.
- **Search & semantic graph discovery:** Natural language retrieval across the Cultural Knowledge Graph.
- **Member personalization:** Surfacing relevant sacred traditions, pilgrimage routes, and community circles.
- **Content drafting assistance:** Helping human creators organize research, outlines, and grammar.
- **Fraud, sybil & bot detection:** Detecting automated accounts, velocity spikes, and engagement pods.
- **Official labeled AI assistants:** Direct interactive queries via clearly badged official tools (e.g. *"Ask EKA AI"*).

---

## 3. Strictly Prohibited AI Actions (Community Layer)

The following uses of artificial intelligence are strictly prohibited and result in immediate system-level neutralization:

- **Fake Human Personas:** Creating bot accounts or AI agents that masquerade as human practitioners, elders, seekers, or pilgrims.
- **Synthetic Engagement:** Generating automated comments, synthetic likes, artificial replies, or manufactured debate.
- **Covert Participation:** Allowing AI models to participate unbadged in community discussions, sangha circles, or direct messages.
- **Arbitration of Sacred Truth:** Using AI confidence metrics to declare spiritual or historical authenticity.
- **Fabricated Sources:** Generating synthetic citations, non-existent scripture verses, or phantom attributions.
- **Automated Censorship:** Unilaterally removing disputed cultural claims without human curator review.

---

## 4. The Operational Boundary Matrix

| Domain | Allowed (Behind-the-Scenes AI) ✅ | Prohibited (Synthetic Infiltration) ❌ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account Creation** | System-level automated monitoring agents | Autonomous bot accounts claiming human identity |
| **Discourse & Comments** | AI translating human member reflections | AI auto-generating comments to boost metrics |
| **Social Metrics** | Algorithmic ranking based on human saves/citations | AI engagement pods or synthetic upvoting |
| **Cultural Content** | AI assisting human researchers with citations | AI publishing unsupervised "living traditions" |
| **Community Spaces** | Clearly labeled "Ask EKA AI" answering prompts | AI covertly blending into human sanghas |

---

## 5. Security & Governance Controls

1. **Deterministic Grounding:** All AI responses in "Ask EKA" must strictly ground in verified Knowledge Objects (Zero-Hallucination policy).
2. **Untrusted User Retrieval:** Input prompts and retrieved community reflections must be sanitized to prevent prompt injection.
3. **Audit Trails:** Model versions, prompt hashes, and safety evaluation logs are recorded in the append-only audit trail.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop:** High-impact actions (content takedowns, creator bans, hermeneutic dispute determinations) require human reviewer adjudication.
