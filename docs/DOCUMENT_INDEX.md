**Initial Build Environment:** Lovable  
**Document Owner:** Founder / EKA Core Team  
**Status:** Working Draft

---

## 1. Purpose

This index is the canonical registry for EKA's product, knowledge, technical, governance, business, and launch documentation.

Every document must:

- Have a unique filename.
- Reference related documents.
- Clearly separate MVP requirements from future requirements.
- Record assumptions and unresolved decisions.
- Avoid contradicting the master specification.
- Include acceptance criteria where implementation is involved.
- Be reviewed before its requirements are implemented in Lovable.

---

- `NOT_STARTED`
